Na Saúde e na Doença - Podcast

June 01, 2026

I collaborated with Guilherme Geirinhas, Margarida Santos and Maria Morango (art director) to create a 3D visualization of the set for the podcast "Na Saúde e na Doença." The set is divided into two areas: a workspace separated by a door, representing the more clinical and private conversation space, and a living room where guests are received for casual discussions. The project combines the perspective of a healthcare professional with the humorous view of a hypochondriac to explore everyday and societal topics. You can find all episodes on the podcast's YouTube channel: Na Saúde e na Doença
