WORTEN MOCK FEST 2026 - 3D Mockup

July / August 2026

For the WORTEN MOCK FEST, I was responsible for the 3D visualization of the main stage and festival area, collaborating closely with the producer to bring his vision to life. I created a simplified 3D construction of the venue to help visualize the future event, making adjustments to the different areas whenever necessary. The project included detailed renders, layout planning, a 360º interactive experience to facilitate visualization.

WORTEN MOCK FEST Official Website

Below you can explore the 360º virtual tour of the festival area. The three dots in the bottom right corner allow you to directly choose a location. The "Top View" button lets you see the avenue and explore the exterior area in front of Cinema São Jorge.

360º Virtual Tour - Cinema São Jorge (Worten MOCK Fest)

Live Event

The event took place on August 28, 29, and 30, 2026, featuring a wide range of comedians. The lineup included well-known Portuguese acts such as the reunion of the Gato Fedorento trio (Ricardo Araújo Pereira, Miguel Góis, and José Diogo Quintela) in "Assim Vamos ter de Falar de Outra Maneira", Hugo Sousa, and Guilherme Duarte, among others. In the international section, comedians like Chris D'Elia, making his debut in Portugal, Michelle Wolf, and Sam Morril were highlights of the festival.
